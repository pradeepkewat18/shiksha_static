27 Jan 2025

On the auspicious occasion of Akshay Tritiya, we are excited to launch Shikshacart, India’s Premier Scholarship Platform. This day, blessed by Goddess Saraswati, the symbol of education, and Goddess Lakshmi, the symbol of wealth, is a great time to launch a platform that helps students.

At Shikshacart, we aim to provide students not only with access to quality education but also with the financial support they need to pursue their academic career. By offering scholarships and valuable study material, we are here to help ambitious students take the next step towards their educational journey. Whether you’re in primary education or advancing your studies, Shikshacart is here to support you.

Registrations on our platform, Shikshacart, will open in the first week of June, so don’t miss this opportunity to unlock your scholarship and take the next step in your academic journey.

Our Goals -

  1. We want to build India's first platform where every student, either from a village or a big city, gets an equal chance to study, stand on their own feet, and support their family.
  2. We encourage students to earn their scholarships through their own hard work and make their parents' efforts lighter by taking one step closer to their dreams.
  3. Through this platform, we are committed to making education equal for every child in India by breaking all barriers of caste, religion, and background, ensuring that whether a student studies in a government or private academy , or belongs to SC, ST, OBC, or General category, they all get the same opportunities to win their Scholarship and access to quality education without discrimination.
  4. In the next two years, Shikshacart aims to cover 40% of areas in India where education is currently lacking. We are on a mission to make education accessible to every child in need by sponsoring the complete education of deserving students, allowing them to focus on their studies and pursue the best courses for their careers, all without financial barriers.

How will achieve this goal :

  1. We will gather students from both rural and urban areas through an easy-to-use platform, which provides scholarships & learning materials to make education accessible for all. By partnering with Academic bodies, NGOs, and community groups, we’ll ensure no students are missing the opportunity they deserve through ShikshaCart.
  2. To help students prepare effectively for scholarship exams, our platform provides topic-wise study materials, continuous learning modules, and regular practice test papers—all accessible after registration. With these resources, students can achieve success through their own hard work and dedication, fulfill their parents' dreams, and move confidently toward a brighter future.
  3. Through our platform, we aim to create true equality in education by breaking all barriers of caste, religion, and background. Every student, whether from a government or private school/College, or from SC, ST, OBC, or General category, will get the same opportunity to prepare for scholarships through their own hard work. To achieve this, we are building a transparent and open competition system where only performance will matter, not background. Every student will be judged fairly based on their efforts and giving everyone a truly equal chance to fulfill their parents' dreams.
  4. To achieve our goal of covering 40% of under-educated areas in India within the next two years, Shikshacart will actively partner with local communities, NGOs, and government bodies to identify deserving students in underprivileged regions. We will provide full scholarships, covering all educational expenses, and ensure that these children receive access to quality courses and learning resources. Through targeted outreach programs, skill development workshops, and continuous support, we will create a pathway for these students to pursue their dreams and achieve success without financial barriers.

Future Impact of Shikshacart on education-

  1. Through ShikshaCart scholarships, many deserving students, whose families do not fully support or prioritize education, will finally get the chance to complete their studies and move confidently towards a better future.
  2. In the coming years, more families in under-aware areas will start valuing education as the true foundation of growth, and students will proudly continue their learning without feeling it is a burden.
  3. As more deserving students receive the right opportunities at the right time, society will begin to see a powerful shift where talent, dreams, and hard work define success, not background or financial status.
