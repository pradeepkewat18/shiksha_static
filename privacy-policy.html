Privacy Policy

At Shikshacart, we are dedicated to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website or register through our platform.

Collection of Personal Information

When you use our platform, we collect specific personal details including your full name, email address, mobile number, Aadhaar number or scanned copy, and previous academic records such as school or college mark sheets and certificates.This information is collected during registration to confirm your identity and eligibility for educational services or opportunities provided through Shikshacart.

Data Recipients, Transfer, and Disclosure of Personal Information

Your information may be shared with relevant academic institutions, verification agencies, or government bodies solely for the purpose of document validation and identity verification.

We do not sell, rent, or misuse your personal data. Disclosure to any third party is strictly limited to service-related obligations or as required by law enforcement.

Use of Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us personalize your experience. They enable us to remember your preferences, improve navigation, reduce load times, and offer you a smoother browsing experience across pages.

Payment Gateway

All online payments are handled through secure and authorized payment gateways integrated with our platform. We do not store your debit/credit card numbers or payment credentials. The gateways we use are PCI-DSS compliant, ensuring your financial data remains protected.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use tracking technologies like browser cookies, pixels, or Google Analytics to understand website usage trends. These tools collect non-personal data like device type, IP address, session time, and clicked pages, which help us analyze user behavior and improve performance.

Data Security

We employ administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information.This includes secure server hosting, data encryption, firewalls, access restrictions, and regular system updates to safeguard your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external websites for additional resources or references.

We do not have control over the content or privacy practices of these third-party sites, and visiting them is at your discretion.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or revise this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice.

Any changes will be effective upon posting to our website, and we encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our services implies acceptance of any updates.
